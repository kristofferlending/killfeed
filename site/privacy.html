Privacy

Last updated 23 September 2026

The short version. KillFeed runs on your own computer. It has no account, no login and no telemetry. Your recordings and the clips it makes never leave your machine, and we never see them.

What the app does with your files

KillFeed reads the video files in the folder you point it at, looks for kill-feed text in the picture, and writes finished clips to the output folder you chose. Settings, the clip ledger and the log live in %APPDATA%\KillFeed on your PC. All of this is local. Nothing is copied to us, and there is no server involved.

What leaves your computer

Almost nothing, and only when you ask for it:

  • Check for updates. If you press it, the app asks GitHub whether a newer release exists. GitHub sees that request, as it would if you opened the page in a browser.
  • Send feedback. This creates a zip on your desktop containing the log and the text the OCR read – never video, never your clips. Nothing is sent anywhere. You decide whether to post it in our Discord.

The app has no other network access. If you join our Discord server, that is Discord's service and their privacy policy applies.

YouTube and Google data

The repository also contains an optional upload script that is not part of KillFeed.exe and is not used unless you set it up yourself with your own Google credentials. If you do, it uses the YouTube Data API to upload clips to your own channel and, if you enable it, the YouTube Analytics API to read view statistics for your own videos so that clip selection can learn from them.

The sign-in token is stored in a file on your own computer and is used only to talk to Google on your behalf. It is never transmitted to us, and we have no access to your channel. You can revoke access at any time in your Google account permissions, and deleting token.json removes it from your machine.

KillFeed's use of information received from Google APIs follows the Google API Services User Data Policy, including the Limited Use requirements. Data obtained from Google APIs is used only to provide the features described above, is not sold, is not transferred to anyone, and is not used for advertising.

This website

killfeed.no is a single static page hosted by Vercel. It sets no cookies, runs no analytics and has no mailing list. Our host records ordinary server request data such as your IP address for operating and securing the site. The page embeds one video from YouTube: if you play it, YouTube may set its own cookies, under Google's privacy policy.

Children

KillFeed is not directed at children under 13 and we knowingly collect no information from anyone.

Changes and contact

If this policy changes, the date at the top changes with it. Questions: ask in #setup-help on Discord, or open an issue on GitHub. KillFeed is made by Lending Equipment, Solund, Norway.

What it does

Everything happens after your session, once the game is closed. You get a Windows notification when something new is ready.

01

Reads the kill feed

OCR on the HUD text in your own recordings – OBS, ShadowPlay, Medal, Aitum. Kills, vehicles destroyed, distance and victim.

02

Cuts the good bits

15–30 s shorts around each kill wave, in 9:16 with a title. Long runs are split at the natural pauses. The same kill from two recordings becomes one clip.

03

Builds a daily recap

A chronological 16:9 recap of the day's kills from your normal recordings, 5–10 minutes, built automatically when enough is collected.

Get started

Three steps, once.

STEP 1

Download and run

Grab KillFeed.exe. No installer. First start takes a few seconds.

STEP 2

Answer four questions

Where your recordings are, what to make, what is good enough to publish, and where the clips should go. A OneDrive or Google Drive folder syncs them to your phone.

STEP 3

Play

KillFeed lives in the tray, starts with Windows, and waits until you close the game before it clips – no CPU stolen mid-match.

FAQ

Short answers. Longer ones on Discord.

Is anything uploaded?

No. Everything runs on your PC. There is no account and no server. Your clips stay in the folder you chose until you post them yourself.

Windows says "unrecognized app" – is it safe?

That is SmartScreen reacting to an unsigned exe. The alpha is not code-signed yet (that comes with the first paid version). Click More info → Run anyway. The source is open on GitHub if you want to check.

What do I need?

Windows 10/11, WARDOGS recordings from any tool (OBS, NVIDIA ShadowPlay, Medal, Aitum Vertical, Xbox Game Bar) at 1080p or 1440p with the HUD at 100 %. The Recording guide in the app has settings for each.

Does it work for infantry?

Yes – kills, vehicle kills and distance are read the same way. The lead-in before a kill is 8 s by default; tell us on Discord if that is too short for your style.

What does it cost?

The alpha is free. A Pro version (no watermark, 1440p, your own logo) is planned once the alpha has proven itself. Alpha testers will hear about it first on Discord.

Something went wrong

Press Send feedback in the app – it puts a zip with the log and text hits (never video) on your desktop. Drop it in #feedback on Discord.
